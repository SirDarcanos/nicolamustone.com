**Carbonara.Rocks** is built around a firm opinion: cream and onions do not belong in carbonara.

The traditional Italian recipe needs five ingredients: pasta, eggs, Pecorino Romano, guanciale, and black pepper. Its silky sauce comes from technique, not cream. Egg yolks, rendered guanciale fat, and starchy pasta water are mixed away from direct heat to create the emulsion.

The site explains what to use, what to leave out, and how each step contributes to the result. It is opinionated without pretending everyone has access to the same ingredients. Guanciale and Pecorino are not available in every shop, so the recipe includes practical alternatives such as bacon or a milder blend of cheeses. You can still cook a very good carbonara tonight with what you can find.

The site itself is deliberately small: one static page, with no database or runtime. It loads quickly and remains easy to follow on a phone, even when the pan is hot and you have one greasy thumb available.

The strong opinions give Carbonara.Rocks its character. Everything else is there to help you get dinner onto the plate.

## Notable bits

- Defends the authentic Italian carbonara—no cream, no compromises.
- Allowed variations for when guanciale or Pecorino aren’t around.
- One static page—instant loads, nothing to wait for.
- Single-minded by design: one dish, done right.
