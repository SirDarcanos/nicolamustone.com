**MediEvil.org** is a guide to the wider MediEvil community. It brings together links that would otherwise be spread across wikis, forums, Discord servers, modding sites, and repositories.

From one page, visitors can find **Gallowpedia**, the community forums, Speedrun.com, speedrunning guides, and the speedrunning Discord. It also points to active communities such as **Cult of Fortesque**, the MediEvil decompilation project, the **FrogLord** modding tools, mods published on GameBanana, and places where the games can still be purchased.

The site does not try to replace any of those projects. They already have their own maintainers and communities. MediEvil.org gives them a stable, memorable signpost, helping new players and returning fans find the right place to begin.

There is also a small contact form. Visitors can use it to report a broken link, suggest a resource, ask where to find something, or get pointed toward the right part of the community. Sometimes a person can provide a better introduction than a directory of links.

MediEvil.org is an unofficial, non-profit project created by a fan. It is not affiliated with Sony; it exists to make Gallowmere and the people keeping it alive easier to find.

## Notable bits

- Every corner of the fandom in one place—wiki, forums, Discords, speedruns, mods.
- A signpost, not a platform: it links out, hosts nothing.
- A help form for anyone who lands lost.
